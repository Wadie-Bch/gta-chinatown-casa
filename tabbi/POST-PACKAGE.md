## Post timing

Comedy shorts peak on weekday evenings in the viewer's timezone. Your largest
English-speaking audiences are US East and UK. Publish at **17:00 UTC Tuesday
or Thursday** — that is 13:00 US East, 18:00 UK, 18:00 Casablanca. Avoid Friday
and Saturday; discovery is worse for new channels.

Schedule rather than publishing live, so the thumbnail and description are
already final when the video goes out.

## What is still open

- The four character voices are not in this cut. The dialogue is on screen and
  each character has a synthesised vocal identity instead. Adding real voices
  means re-rendering the audio only, not the picture.
- The logo is the code-drawn tab creature. If you replace it, the mark lives in
  one function: `tabbiMark()` in `src/chars/tabbi.js`.
