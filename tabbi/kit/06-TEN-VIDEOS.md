# Ten episodes, in launch order

Each is five minutes, built on the existing engine, and each cuts into 4–6
Shorts. Ranked by how well the premise travels to a stranger who has never
heard of you.

The two marked **BUILT** already exist as finished 300-second films in the old
style. They need a re-render in the new style, not a rebuild.

---

**1. "He Ordered Pizza. The Browser Said No." — BUILT**
He has exactly enough money for one pizza. He has to prove he is human first.
He fails. His robot assistant passes. The pizza is delivered to the robot.
*Hook: a CAPTCHA that says TOO CORRECT.*
*Shorts: the CAPTCHA, the tab-scan, the identity swap, the share-link ending.*

**2. "He Tried to Cancel. They Tried to Be Nice." — BUILT**
Eleven months of a subscription for one episode watched once. The cancel link
moves every time he clicks it. A retention specialist will not stop being kind.
*Hook: the cancel button running away.*
*Shorts: the moving link, the accordion, the offer wall, the hold room.*

**3. "The Password Rules Changed Again."**
Eight characters. One number. One symbol. Not your last twelve passwords. Not a
word. Not a not-word. He ends up with something he cannot type twice.
*Hook: "PASSWORD CANNOT CONTAIN A PASSWORD."*

**4. "He Clicked Accept All."**
The cookie banner was one click. Then four hundred vendors physically move into
his tab, one by one, and start taking measurements.
*Hook: the room filling with strangers with clipboards.*

**5. "The Free Trial Requires a Card."**
Free. Just needs a card. And a phone number. And a photo of the card. And a
photo of you holding the card. And a reason.
*Hook: escalating verification for something free.*

**6. "Your Call Is Important To Us."**
Trapped in a phone tree with no exit. Press 1 for options. Press 2 for these
options again. Gate is the menu and he is enjoying himself.
*Hook: pressing 9 and arriving back at the start, older.*

**7. "The Update Is Mandatory."**
He is trying to do one thing. Something wants to restart. It negotiates. Then
it stops negotiating.
*Hook: "RESTART NOW / REMIND ME IN 4 MINUTES."*

**8. "He Left One Star."**
A one-star review triggers a customer service response so relentlessly warm it
becomes a siege. RETAIN returns.
*Hook: "We are so sorry. May we call you? May we come over?"*

**9. "Delivery Attempted."**
The notice says a delivery was attempted. He was home. He was watching the
door. The tracking map disagrees with physics.
*Hook: the driver's dot passing through his building without stopping.*

**10. "Are You Still Watching?"**
He is. He says so. It asks again. It keeps asking until the question becomes
existential and he has to answer honestly.
*Hook: the same dialog, five times, getting more personal.*

---

## Which to post first

**Not #1.** Post the Shorts from #1 first, then the full episode once you have
an audience with a reason to stay five minutes.

Of the Shorts, lead with **the CAPTCHA "TOO CORRECT" beat**. It is the most
universally recognised situation you have, it has a hard punchline, and it
works with no setup at all.
