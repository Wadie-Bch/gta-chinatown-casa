# The name

You said TABBI is taken. The character is changing to a cursor anyway, so the
name should change with it.

## What a good name has to do here

- **4–6 letters.** It has to fit a thumbnail corner and a phone keyboard.
- **End in a vowel.** Globally pronounceable — a Moroccan, a Brazilian and a
  Filipino should say it the same way on first sight.
- **Mean nothing.** A made-up word is trademarkable and searchable. A real word
  is neither.
- **Read as a creature**, not a product. You are naming a character, and the
  channel takes the character's name.

## Candidates, ranked

| name | handle | why | risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **KLIKO** | `@kliko` | click + -o. Reads as a creature instantly. Five letters, one hard consonant, impossible to mispronounce. | may be taken; "Kliko" is a Dutch waste-bin brand, unrelated sector |
| **NUBBO** | `@nubbo` | soft, toy-like, very ownable | less obviously about a cursor |
| **TAPPO** | `@tappo` | tap + -o, good for mobile-native | Italian word for "cap/cork" |
| **ARRO** | `@arro` | arrow, shortened. Clean and modern. | short enough that the handle is probably gone |
| **KURSO** | `@kurso` | cursor, phonetic | reads slightly like "course" in Spanish |
| **BLIPO** | `@blipo` | blip + -o, sound-led | least connected to the character |

**My pick: KLIKO.** It is the only one where someone who has never seen the
channel can hear the name, see the character, and connect them in one second.

## Verify it yourself — 5 minutes, do not skip

For each candidate, in order. Stop at the first one that passes everything.

1. `youtube.com/@kliko` — if it loads a channel, it is taken.
2. `tiktok.com/@kliko` — same.
3. `instagram.com/kliko` — same.
4. Google `"kliko"` — is there a big company on page one in tech or media?
5. `kliko.com` and `kliko.tv` in any registrar — not required, but knowing
   costs you nothing.
6. Search the **EUIPO** and **WIPO** trademark databases for the word in
   class 41 (entertainment). Free, and it is the difference between a brand
   and a problem later.

If a name fails only on the domain, take it anyway. If it fails on trademark in
entertainment, drop it and move to the next.

## Claim it everywhere the same day

Even the platforms you will not use for months. Handles are free and they are
gone forever once someone else takes them. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X,
Threads. Fifteen minutes, permanent value.
