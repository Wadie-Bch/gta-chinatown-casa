# The 2026 style — as numbers, not adjectives

Your critique was right. The old look was Apple-system-settings: warm white,
thin lines, one muted accent. Premium on a desktop, invisible in a feed.

The fix is not "more colour". It is **contrast, weight and hardness.**

## The rules

| element | old | new |
|---|---|---|
| outline weight | 5–6 px | **10–12 px** |
| shadow | soft blur, 18% black | **solid ink, offset 16–20 px, no blur** |
| background | one warm white everywhere | **saturated colour block per scene** |
| accent | one mint | **six saturated hues, one dominant per scene** |
| display type | 700 weight, no stroke | **900 weight, 16 px ink stroke, hard offset shadow** |
| character vs ground | both light | **light character on saturated ground, always** |

## Palette

```
ink     #14141A   outlines, type, shadows — never pure black
paper   #FFF4E4   the character's body, warm not white
lime    #B9FF39   the signature. one element per frame, never two.
mint    #00E39A   go / success
coral   #FF5A5F   refusal / error
violet  #8A5CFF   systems, interfaces, authority
sun     #FFC93C   money, food, want
sky     #3ACBFF   calm, waiting, cold
```

One saturated hue owns each scene. The character stays `paper` so he pops out
of every one of them. That single rule is what makes a feed thumbnail work.

## The character — KLIKO

A cursor with a face. Chibi proportions: **the head is the arrow and it is
roughly 60% of his height.** The body is a small rounded box underneath, with
thin limbs.

- The head is **tilted about −12°**. Every cursor ever drawn is tilted. That
  tilt is the entire recognition cue — without it he is a triangle.
- **The face stays level** even when the head tilts. Counter-rotate it.
- Eyes are large, ink-filled, with one white highlight each.
- A lime click-ring badge sits near the tip. One accent. Not a stripe across
  the face — that read as a blindfold in the first pass.
- Hard ink shadow offset down-right, under everything, no blur.

## Type

Display text is 900 weight with a 16 px ink stroke and a 7 px hard shadow, so
it survives being 200 px wide on a phone. Body dialogue keeps its opaque plate
— that rule was already right and does not change.

## What this costs to apply

The character files and the palette. Roughly one working pass over
`src/chars/`, `src/core/palette.js` and `src/core/draw.js`, then a full
re-render of both episodes. The shot code, the timing, the camera work, the
story and the audio are all untouched — that is the benefit of having kept
them as data.
