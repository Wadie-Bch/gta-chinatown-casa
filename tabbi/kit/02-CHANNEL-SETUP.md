## Settings that matter
- **Country: Morocco.** Do not fake it. It affects nothing about reach and
  faking it risks your AdSense payout later.
- **Language: English.** Set it explicitly.
- **Made for kids: NO** — at the channel level and on every video. Setting it
  to yes disables comments, end screens, notifications and personalised ads.
  It would cripple the channel.
- **Upload defaults**: set the description template, tags and category once so
  every upload starts pre-filled.
