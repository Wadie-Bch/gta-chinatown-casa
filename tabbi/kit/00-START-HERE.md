# CHANNEL KIT — read this first

Everything here assumes one thing: **nothing gets uploaded until the character
style is locked.** That is the right call and it is the first item below.

## What I cannot do for you

I have no access to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram. **I cannot check whether a
name or handle is free.** Every name in `01-NAME.md` is a candidate, not a
confirmed available handle. The 5-minute check is written out for you there.
Do it before you commit to anything.

I also have no audio output, so I have never heard the soundtrack. You are the
only person who can judge whether a joke lands on the beat.

## Today, in this order

1. **Approve or reject the style** — look at `style-2026.png`. Thick outlines,
   hard offset shadows, saturated colour blocks, chunky type with a heavy
   stroke. If you want changes, say them now; every later change costs a
   re-render of everything.
2. **Pick a name and check it** — `01-NAME.md`, 5 minutes.
3. **Create the channel** — `02-CHANNEL-SETUP.md`. Handle, description,
   banner, about, links. Twenty minutes.
4. **Do not upload yet.** The two finished episodes are in the old muted style
   and the old character. They are proof the pipeline works, not launch
   material.

## Then, this week

5. Rebuild the character and palette in the new style (one pass over
   `src/chars/` and `src/core/palette.js`).
6. Re-render Episode 1 and Episode 2 in the new style.
7. Cut 6–8 Shorts out of them.
8. Launch with Shorts, not with the five-minute film. Reasons in
   `05-GROWTH.md` — this is the single most important strategic call in the kit.
